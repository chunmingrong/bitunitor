Get control of your data

Protect your digital privacy

 

Get all the benefits of the data economy with the control you need to protect your privacy.

 

Your data is valuable, but so is your privacy.the data-driven world, we often don’t know who is using our data and why. Businesses need customer data to provide the service we expect and thrive in a competitive market. We want to digitally share things with family, friends and organizations we trust. But our privacy keeps being compromised time-after-time.

 

bitUnitor has world leading expertise in developing innovative blockchain solutions that empower users to securely share their data when, how, and with whom they choose through our intuitive app interface.

 
 

FLEXIBILITY

Share your data with businesses and individuals in a way that works for you and your preferences - manageable anytime, anywhere.

CONTROL

Control exactly how your data is shared - lock down how and when it can be accessed and who can access it. You can also redact or remove historic data at any time.

PRIVACY

Your data is encrypted end-to-end and remains completely anonymous while the processing is verified by trusted organisations.

VISIBILITY

You have complete visibility over which organisations have access to your data, when they have accessed it and how they have used it.

TradeRES

The objective of this project is to develop and test an innovative electricity market design that can meet society’s needs in a near 100% renewable power system. At bitUnitor, we're contributing to the TradeRES project, which aims to develop and test new electricity market designs suitable for a near-100% renewable power system. Our role involves sharing our knowledge on blockchain-based solutions for trading in local energy systems. We're also leading Work Package 6, focusing on engaging stakeholders with the project's open-access tools. Additionally, we coordinate Task 5.2, where we assess the performance of current and new market designs and trading mechanisms for local energy communities in Case Study A. This project has received funding from the European Union’s Horizon 2020 research and innovation program under grant agreement 864276.
